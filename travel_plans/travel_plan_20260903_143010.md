## Detailed Day‑by‑Day Narrative  

### Day 1 – Arrival in Guwahati  
- **Morning:** Land at Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport. Private taxi (or pre‑booked airport transfer) to hotel.  
- **Afternoon:** Rest, then head to **Umananda Island** (short ferry ride) for a gentle walk and panoramic views of the Brahmaputra.  
- **Evening:** Sunset cruise on the Brahmaputra (30 min) followed by dinner at **Mohan’s** (authentic Assamese cuisine).  

### Day 2 – Kaziranga National Park  
- **5 am:** Depart for Kaziranga (pre‑booked private car with driver).  
- **8 am – 12 pm:** Jeep Safari in the **Central/Western Range** – spot one‑horned rhinos, tigers, wild water buffalo, and a chorus of birds.  
- **Lunch:** At the lodge (they serve fresh river fish and Assamese thali).  
- **2 pm – 4 pm:** Optional Elephant Safari (great for close‑up wildlife).  
- **Evening:** Walk around the lodge’s garden, enjoy a bonfire, and stargaze.  

### Day 3 – Shillong – “Scotland of the East”  
- **Morning:** Breakfast, then drive to Shillong via **Shillong‑Guwahati Highway** (picturesque tea gardens, rolling hills).  
- **Afternoon:** Check‑in, lunch at **Mohan’s** (Shillong). Explore **Police Bazar** – try momos, local cheese (chura), and shop for hand‑loom shawls.  
- **Evening:** Head to **Umiam Lake** (also called Barapani) for a paddle‑boat ride and sunset photo‑ops.  

### Day 4 – Cherrapunjee (Sohra) – Waterfall Wonderland  
- **Early start (6 am):** Drive to Cherrapunjee.  
- **Stops:**  
  - **Nohkalikai Falls** – the world‑famous plunge waterfall (30 m drop).  
  - **Mawsmai Cave** – guided walk through limestone chambers.  
  - **Seven Sisters Falls** – a cascade of seven streams.  
  - **Thangkharang Park** – viewpoint over the valley.  
- **Lunch:** Local restaurant (try “pumaloi” – a rice cake).  
- Return to Shillong for a relaxed evening.  

### Day 5 – Mawsynram & Mawlynnong – The Wettest & Cleanest Villages  
- **Morning:** Drive to **Mawsynram** (record‑holding rainfall). Short trek to the **Living Root Bridge** near **Nongriat** (requires a 2‑hour hike each way; optional for the adventurous).  
- **Mid‑day:** Continue to **Mawlynnong** – walk the bamboo bridge, visit the **Sky Walk** (transparent glass platform), and see the **Waterfall**.  
- **Evening:** Stay in a homestay; enjoy a traditional Khasi dinner (pork with bamboo shoots, rice).  

### Day 6 – Jorhat & Majuli Island – Tea & Culture  
- **Morning:** Early drive to **Jorhat** (or take the **Assam Express** train for a scenic ride).  
- **Tea Estate Tour:** Visit **Tocklai Tea Estate** – learn about tea plucking, processing, and tasting.  
- **Afternoon:** Board a ferry at **Nagaon** to **Majuli** (the ferry runs every 30 min).  
- **Majuli Highlights:**  
  - **Satras** – especially **Satras of Auniati, Dakhinpat, and Garamur** (art, dance, religious rituals).  
  - **Mating Deer Park** – feed the gentle deer.  
  - **Handicraft Workshops** – watch traditional mask making.  
- **Sunset:** From **Bordowa**, watch the Brahmaputra’s golden glow.  

### Day 7 – Return to Guwahati & Departure  
- **Morning:** Ferry back to the mainland, drive to Guwahati.  
- **Umananda Island** (if missed on Day 1) – quick visit.  
- **Shopping:** **Fancy Bazaar** for silk, tea, and Assamese jewellery.  
- **Transfer:** Hotel to airport for your onward flight.

## Practical Tips & Logistics  

| Item | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| **Best Travel Window** | **October – March** – pleasant weather, clear skies, and wildlife visibility. |
| **Visas** | Indian e‑Visa (tourist) is sufficient for most nationalities. |
| **Transport** | Hire a **reliable 4‑WD SUV** with a driver (most agencies in Guwahati offer daily rates ₹3,500‑₹5,000). Public buses are available but slower and less comfortable. |
| **Internal Flights** | Not necessary for a 7‑day loop; road travel keeps the itinerary flexible. |
| **Health** | Carry basic meds (anti‑malaria tablets if you’re traveling from a high‑risk area), insect repellent, and a small first‑aid kit. |
| **Currency** | Indian Rupee (₹). ATMs are common in Guwahati, Shillong, and Jorhat; carry cash for small towns. |
| **Clothing** | Light layers, waterproof jacket, comfortable trekking shoes, and a hat. Evenings can be chilly in the hills. |
| **Connectivity** | 4G coverage is good in Guwahati, Shillong, and major towns. In remote villages (Mawlynnong, Majuli) rely on offline maps. |
| **Cultural Etiquette** | Respect local customs – remove shoes before entering homes/temples, ask permission before photographing people, and avoid discussing politics. |
| **Safety** | The region is generally safe for tourists. Keep copies of ID, travel insurance, and emergency contacts. |
