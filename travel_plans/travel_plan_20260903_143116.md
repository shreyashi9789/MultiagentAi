## 🗓️ Itinerary
## 7‑Day North‑East India Adventure  
**Theme:** Nature, culture & adventure – a mix of waterfalls, hills, tribal villages, wildlife and iconic festivals.  
**Base airport:** **Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (GAU), Guwahati** – the most convenient gateway to the entire region.  

> **Quick tip:** If you’re flying from outside India, the easiest international connection is via Delhi, Kolkata or Bangkok to Guwahati (≈2 h flight).  

---

### DAY‑BY‑DAY BREAKDOWN  

| Day | Region / Hub | Morning | Mid‑day | Evening | Overnight |
|-----|--------------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|
| **1** | **Guwahati (Assam)** | Arrive in Guwahati, transfer to hotel. <br>Visit **Kamakhya Temple** (one of the 51 Shakti Peethas). | Lunch at **Mising Kitchen** (traditional Assamese thali). <br>Explore **Umananda Island** (short ferry ride, scenic views of Brahmaputra). | Sunset cruise on the Brahmaputra River; dinner at **Paradise** (riverfront). | **Hotel:** *The Guwahati Grand* (mid‑range) or *Radisson Blu* (luxury). |
| **2** | **Kaziranga National Park (Assam)** | Early‑morning drive (≈4 h) to Kaziranga. <br>Check‑in & brief rest. | **Elephant‑back safari** (or jeep) in **Zone 1** – spot the one‑horned rhinoceros, tigers, wild water buffalo. | Return to lodge, enjoy a **Bihu‑style cultural show**. | **Resort:** *Kaziranga Wilderness* or *Bison Resort* (eco‑friendly). |
| **3** | **Shillong (Meghalaya)** | Drive to Shillong (≈5 h, scenic hills). <br>Stop at **Umiam Lake** for photo‑ops. | Lunch at **City Hut** (local Khasi dishes). <br>Visit **Don Bosco Museum** – excellent intro to NE tribal cultures. | Stroll through **Police Bazar**, try **Jadoh** (rice‑meat pilaf). <br>Evening walk to **Elephant Falls** (lit at night). | **Hotel:** *The Heritage Club* or *Hotel Centre Point* (budget‑friendly). |
| **4** | **Cherrapunji (Meghalaya)** | Early departure to Cherrapunji (≈2 h). <br>Visit **Nohkalikai Falls** (tallest plunge waterfall in India). | Picnic lunch near **Mawsmai Cave**; explore the limestone caves. | Sunset at **Laitlum Canyons** – breathtaking valley views. | **Homestay:** *Mawphlang Homestay* (authentic tribal experience) or *Polo Orchid Resort*. |
| **5** | **Mawsynram & Dawki (Meghalaya)** | Drive to **Mawsynram** (world’s wettest place) – short trek to the **Mawsynram Viewpoint**. | Lunch at a local **Jaintia** family home (home‑cooked thali). <br>Proceed to **Dawki** on the India‑Bangladesh border – boat ride on the crystal‑clear **Umngot River**. | Return to Shillong, relax at **Police Bazar** for street‑food tasting (pani‑puri, momos). | Same hotel in Shillong. |
| **6** | **Gangtok (Sikkim)** | Fly from Guwahati to **Bagdogra** (≈1 h) → drive to Gangtok (≈4 h). <br>Check‑in & freshen up. | Lunch at **Baker’s Café** (Western & Indian). <br>Visit **Namgyal Institute of Tibetology** and **Enchey Monastery**. | Evening stroll on **MG Road**, try **Momos** and **Thukpa**. | **Hotel:** *The Elgin* (luxury) or *Hotel Tashi* (mid‑range). |
| **7** | **North Sikkim – Tsomgo & Baba Mandir** | Early‑morning excursion to **Tsomgo Lake** (≈45 km). <br>Optional yak‑ride & yak‑butter tea. | Continue to **Baba Harbhajan Singh Memorial** (high‑altitude shrine). <br>Lunch at a local **Lepcha** homestay (organic bamboo‑cooked dishes). | Return to Gangtok, last‑minute shopping for **hand‑woven pashmina** & **organic tea**. | Depart from Bagdogra (flight to Delhi/your onward destination) or extend stay in Sikkim. |
